## Notes for the new parts
- **shin_cover_front/back_L/R (new, replaces v37_shin_cover_L/R):** chunky clamshell shin block (~56 x 61 x 30 mm vs the 38 x 52 x 25 mm C clip). Fit the back half behind the shin sheets, then the front half; the front half's 1 mm lap lip keys into the back half. Glue (CA or PVA) along the split. The top plate hugs the shin sheets (0.35 mm clearance); the open bottom sits over the top of the boot cuff. Print both halves split face down, no supports (the rounded top is under 45 degrees). The front top edge is chamfered so the thigh knee sheets clear it at full knee bend.
- **torso_shell_front/back:** clean cone. The back runs in a straight line from x -78 at the waist to -63 at the shoulder (v3.7: -91 to -59, vertical then chamfered). No power-switch slot any more.
- **torso_frame:** back rail shortened to z 24; hip deck stops at x -58.4 (at the hip-yaw slings).
- **hip_ring:** follows the narrower cone waist.
- **Electronics repack (no new parts):** the bus board and Pi Zero 2W lean 13.4 degrees forward (parallel to the cone back), standing on the battery pod. The IMU is unchanged. The **power switch moves under the kilt**, beside the battery pod on the robot's left (x -64..-46, y 40.5..48, z -8..11.5): lift the kilt to switch on. The battery is still reached from under the kilt as in v3.7 (tray below the waist, nothing added in front of it).
- **Arms, forearms, boots, cuffs, leaves, hip brackets and sheets** are unchanged (the arm STLs differ only by wood-grain tessellation noise, p99 < 0.05 mm).
