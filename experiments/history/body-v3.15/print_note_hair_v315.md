## Wig - one piece, yellow / blond matte PLA (PETG ~10 C hotter)
- Orientation: as exported: crown axis tilted 48 deg from straight-down (least support volume of 6120 orientations searched); lies on the dome + clump backs. Supports: tree supports (auto, build plate only, threshold 40 deg, 0.2 mm top Z gap) + 5 mm brim. Estimated support ~30 g (tree), print ~12.9 h at P1S standard speed. (Straight crown-down would need ~42 g of support / 206.2 cm2 of overhang - worse.)
- Settings: 0.2 mm layers, 2 walls (0.8 mm), 3 top / 3 bottom layers, sparse infill 5 % LIGHTNING (clumps + crown dome print as sealed hollow shells), 0.4 mm nozzle. The sim mass assumes 2 walls + 8 % infill (heavier than lightning), so the real wig is a few grams lighter than the 177.1 g used for the head-pitch check.
- Pointed tips: 'thick bridges' off, 5 mm brim. Remove the tree supports from the clump undersides with flush cutters; the crown dome is smooth (no seam, no grooves, no bosses) and sits under the felt hat.
- Fit: pin 3 x 3 mm rods (12 mm long; 6 mm glued into the 3 BLIND holes inside the crown dome, 6 mm protruding) into the 3 matching 3.2 mm holes in head_upper; the wig seats with a 0.3 mm gap (seat gap p5/p50/p95 = 0.332/0.44/1.087 mm; p95 over the wood-grain grooves). Glue optional.
- No head_upper reprint (drill option): glue the 3 pins into the wig, dab paint on the pin tips, seat the wig to mark the shell, then drill 3.2 mm at the three marks (2.6 mm wall, nothing behind it there).
- Felt look: 0.25 mm surface ripple on the clumps (prints fine at 0.2 mm); optional ochre dry-brush in the V-grooves.

## head_upper
- Identical to v3.14's (3 crown pin holes). Reprint only if you haven't printed the v3.14 one and prefer printed holes over drilling. Wood brown PLA, 0.2 mm, 3 walls, 15 % gyroid, rim down, tree supports inside only.
