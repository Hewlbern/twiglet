## Notes
- gripper_housing: the box sits on the bed on its inner (body-side) face. Put supports under the long fixed finger tip and in the finger opening. Thin rims (about 1.2 % of the surface under 1.2 mm) are at the edges of the finger opening only, so turn slicer gap-fill on. The box rim is 1 mm below the housing top.
- The box is solid fill between the housing and the box skin. It is mostly under 6 mm thick, so hollowing would save only about 4 cm3. Use 4 walls and 30 % infill.
- boot: the new toe cap is a hollow dome and is not enclosed (the boot is one body). Its underside, plus the rand, adds about 1500 mm2 of >45 deg overhang (3154 vs 1618 mm2 for rev C). Use tree supports from the build plate only, and check the preview.
- The kilt petals are 12 mm longer (100.3 mm tall as printed) and print upside down on the lip, the same as rev C.
- Fit check (rest pose, in sim/CAD): housing vs S32 gripper servo 214.6 mm3, which is identical to rev C (press-fit sleeve). The housing has 0 overlap with forearm, horn and elbow. The finger sweep is clear over the 0..60 deg range. Knee 0..90 deg and ankle +-45 deg sweeps are clear for the shins and boots.
