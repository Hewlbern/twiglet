## Wig - one piece, yellow / blond matte PLA
- Orientation: as exported: crown axis tilted 44 deg from straight-down (least support volume of 6120 orientations searched); lies on the dome + clump backs. Supports: tree supports (auto, build plate only, threshold 40 deg, 0.2 mm top Z gap) + 5 mm brim. ~29 g of tree support, ~11.2 h (P1S standard). (Straight crown-down: ~37 g support / 184.4 cm2 overhang - worse.)
- Settings: 0.2 mm layers, 2 walls (0.8 mm), 3 top / 3 bottom layers, sparse infill 5 % LIGHTNING (the locks print as near-hollow sealed shells; lightning only props the top skins) - the sims assume this ~150 g wig, keep 5 %. Sharp lock points: 5 mm brim, 'thick bridges' off.
- Shape: 15 flowing chunky locks (swept S-curves with a gentle twist, parted fringe sweeping up and out clear of the hearts, eye-level side flares curling out, varied lengths; full-shouldered wedge section) grown out of the crown dome with a smooth (tangent) blend - no seam, step or feather edge on top.

## Attachment (the wig cannot rotate, slide or lift)
- Locate: 4 x 3 mm steel or PLA-filament rods, 12 mm long: glue 6 mm into the 4 blind holes in the wig; the 6 mm ends drop into the 4 vertical 3.2 mm holes in head_upper. All pins are vertical = parallel to the push-on direction (no binding).
- Key: a 2.4 mm wide x 1.1 mm tongue (4 arcs of 70/40/40/40 deg - the long arc at the front, so it only fits one way) on the wig underside sits in the matching 3.0 x 1.0 mm groove in head_upper (0.3 mm side / 0.2 mm bottom glue gap). Groove floor wall 1.47 mm.
- Glue land: the whole wig underside is a seat with a controlled 0.3 mm gap to the head (p5/p50 0.335/0.447 mm); the lower lock ends lift off the head (flare) and are not glued.
- Glue: 2-part epoxy (5-min or 30-min, e.g. Loctite/Araldite) - thin bead on the crown seat band + tongue + pin holes, push down, tape for 1 h. Medium CA also works but is brittle on impact; epoxy fills the 0.2-0.3 mm gap properly. For a removable wig: pins + tongue + 3 dots of E6000 / hot glue.
